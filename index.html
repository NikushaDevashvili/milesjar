HOW IT WORKS

How MilesJar works

  1. 01 It notices you are driving

    Your iPhone's motion sensor tells the app a drive has started. You never press start.

  2. 02 GPS measures the route

    Only while you drive, so the battery lasts the day.

  3. 03 The trip is saved when you park

    A notification shows the miles and what they are worth.

  4. 04 Anything off is easy to fix

    Merge two trips, split one at a stop, or add a drive by hand.
